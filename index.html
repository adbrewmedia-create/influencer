FAQ

Questions creators ask us

What exactly do you build?

A full betting content platform on your own domain: website build, betting content, commercial partnerships, tracking infrastructure, growth strategy and ongoing management. For TomGarrattBets.com all of that was delivered within the first month of launch.

What do I have to do?

Create great content. We handle website development, SEO, content production, affiliate relationships, operator negotiations, tracking technology, compliance management, data analysis and ongoing optimisation.

Who owns the platform and the brand?

You do. The model is built around monetising your audience while you keep full brand ownership and a voice true to you. You control your editorial voice, your content creation and how your brand is represented.

How are the commercials structured?

Agreed up front, in simple terms, before anything is built. You generate ongoing revenue from the customers you introduce, and we report transparently on traffic and revenue.

Have you done this before?

Yes. We build and run live, revenue-generating betting platforms with established media brands and creators, from national newspapers to individual creators, across the UK and US, including The Independent and The Evening Standard.

How do you protect my name?

Only licensed, trusted operators appear on your platform, with responsible-gambling tools and messaging and full regulatory and advertising compliance. We also maintain the validity of every offer, deal and piece of content.
